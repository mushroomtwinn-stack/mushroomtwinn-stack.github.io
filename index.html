KRCC Drive Backup

KRCC Drive Backup is a personal, non-commercial desktop backup setup operated by a single individual. It uses rclone and Google Drive to store and verify copies of personal research archives and related files.

Google Drive access is requested only to carry out owner-authorized file transfers and backup verification. The application is not a public file-sharing service and does not provide access to the owner's research dashboard.

For details about how Google Drive data is handled, read the Privacy Policy.
