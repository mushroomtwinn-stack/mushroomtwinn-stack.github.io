Privacy Policy

Application: KRCC Drive Backup
Effective date: November 9, 2026

Purpose and access

KRCC Drive Backup is a personal-use integration between rclone and the owner's Google Drive account. Depending on the Google Drive OAuth permission granted, rclone can access Drive file contents and metadata to upload research backups, list backed-up files, verify transfers, and retrieve archives for recovery. The owner controls which files are backed up.

Use and storage of Google data

Google Drive data is used only for the owner's local backup, integrity checking, and recovery workflows. Backup copies remain stored in the owner's Google Drive account. Local OAuth credentials are held by the owner's computer for rclone authentication. This public information site does not collect Google Drive files or OAuth tokens.

Sharing

Google user data is not sold, used for advertising, or shared with outside users. Google provides the storage and authentication services requested by the owner. This is not a public backup-sharing service.

Retention and control

The owner controls retention of backups stored in Google Drive. The owner can remove backed-up files in Drive and revoke the application's Google Account access at any time in Google Account settings. Revocation stops future API access until reauthorization.

Contact

For questions about this personal-use application, contact the application owner through the support email listed on the Google OAuth consent screen.
